THE CALMEST UPGRADE OF 2026

Bigger tile, calmer room.

One 24×48 in porcelain tile covers the area of eight 12×12 tiles — and cuts grout lines by more than 60%. Fewer lines, less scrubbing, one quiet continuous plane. Porcelain absorbs 0.5% water or less, so spills stay on top: kitchens, entries and baths are its natural home.
